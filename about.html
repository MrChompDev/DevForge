// milestones

Indie Games That Changed Everything

2011 · Markus Persson

Minecraft

Proved that early-access and community-driven development could create a global phenomenon from a garage project.

2014 · Yacht Club Games

Shovel Knight

A love letter to 8-bit platformers that showed the power of Kickstarter and retro-inspired design.

2017 · Bluehole

PUBG

Sparked the battle royale craze. Built in Unreal Engine by a small team iterating on a mod.

2018 · Innersloth

Among Us

A tiny 2D multiplayer game that found its audience years after release through streaming and community word-of-mouth.
